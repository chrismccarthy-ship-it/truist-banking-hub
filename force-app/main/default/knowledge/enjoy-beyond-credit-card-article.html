Disclosures

Disclosure 1 — Rewards

You may earn Rewards on Eligible Purchases made with your Card Account.

1 Point for each $1.00 spent on all Eligible Purchases — You will earn one (1) Point for each $1.00 in Eligible Purchases. There is no limit to the amount of Points you can earn.

2 Points for each $1.00 spent on restaurant Eligible Purchases — You will earn two (2) Points for each $1.00 in Eligible Purchases at restaurants. Purchases at certain merchants, such as bakeries, convenience stores, specialty markets, and merchants whose primary business is the sale of alcoholic beverages, will not earn two (2) Points but are eligible to earn one (1) Point per $1.00.

3 Points for each $1.00 spent on eligible airline ticket, car rental, or hotel lodging purchases — Non-airline ticket purchases, such as seat upgrades, baggage fees, and onboard amenities, as well as non-lodging purchases made at a hotel such as dining and activities, will not earn three (3) Points but are eligible to earn one (1) Point per $1.00.

Eligible Purchase(s): Refers to purchase Transactions less credit chargebacks, credit losses, delinquency assessments, fees, and charges or other Transactions determined (in Truist's sole reasonable discretion) to be unauthorized. The following are expressly excluded: (1) Cash Advances, (2) Quasi-Cash or Cash-Equivalent items (e.g., wire transfers, cryptocurrency, peer-to-peer payment platform transfers, travelers' checks, money orders, foreign currency, lottery tickets, or gambling chips or wagers), (3) Convenience Checks, (4) Stored Value Cards (e.g., gift cards, prepaid cards, etc.), and (5) the purchase of Rewards incentives.

Disclosure 2 — Airport Security & Travel Experience Statement Credits

You must be an eligible U.S. Visa cardholder to participate. You must complete either the Global Entry® and/or TSA PreCheck® application(s) and pay the application fee(s) with your U.S.-issued Truist Enjoy Beyond Visa Credit Card.

Global Entry® is a U.S. Government program operated by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP). Visa and Truist have no control over the program including, but not limited to, application, approval process or enrollment, fees charged by CBP, and no liability with regards to the Global Entry® program.

TSA PreCheck® is a U.S. Government program administered by the Transportation Security Administration (TSA). Visa and Truist have no control over the TSA PreCheck® program.

Every four (4) years, you can earn a maximum total (in aggregate) of $120.00 in statement credit(s) on eligible transactions: Global Entry® application fee or TSA PreCheck® application fee(s). Please allow six (6) to eight (8) weeks after the fee is charged for the statement credit to post.

You can earn a maximum total (in aggregate) of $100.00 in statement credit(s) per calendar year when you use your card at eligible merchants in these MCC categories: Taxicabs, Limousines and Rideshare Services; Cable, Satellite and Other Pay Television/Radio/Streaming Services; Ticket Agencies and Theatrical Producers (Except Motion Pictures). We rely on the MCC assigned to the merchant to determine eligibility. Please allow six (6) to eight (8) weeks after an Eligible Purchase for the statement credit to post.

Your account must be open or closed within the last ninety (90) days, and not in default at the time of statement credit fulfillment. Visa and Truist reserve the right to modify or cancel this offer at any time and without notice.

Disclosure 3 — Loyalty Cash Bonus

You will receive a 10% Loyalty Cash Bonus added to your Rewards redemption amount when you electronically deposit your Rewards redemption into your eligible Truist personal checking, personal savings, or personal money market account.

If you have a Truist Dimension Checking or Signature Advantage Checking account and also electronically deposit your Rewards redemption into an eligible Truist account, you may receive either a 25% or 50% Loyalty Cash Bonus (based on your banking relationships at Truist); this replaces the 10% bonus.

If you have a Truist One Checking account and also electronically deposit your Rewards redemption into an eligible Truist account, you may receive a 20%, 30%, 40%, or 50% Loyalty Cash Bonus; this replaces the 10% bonus.

Bonus percentages are determined using the highest average monthly balance of your combined eligible consumer deposit and investment balances across the prior three (3) calendar months. Your eligible Loyalty Cash Bonus percentage may increase monthly but decrease only once every three (3) calendar months. If your electronic deposit is rejected for any reason, the Rewards amount redeemed will be refunded to your Rewards Account. You will not earn any Loyalty Cash Bonus on a rejected redemption.
